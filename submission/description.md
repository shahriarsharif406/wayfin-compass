Wayfin Compass helps travellers when money problems and flight problems collide. For a fictional travel-card company, it guides customers through card disputes and flight disruptions instead of only answering FAQs. A pre-chat form captures the issue, urgency and AI-disclosure consent. Guided workflows find the dispute type, list the exact evidence needed, and open a real case with a number and priority through an API action. Airline compensation amounts come from tested code, never a model's guess. Distinctive feature: Evidence from Space. For flight claims, Compass checks NASA satellite events near the airports and NOAA space weather on the flight date, giving customers context for "extraordinary circumstances" without ever changing the verdict. When unsure or urgent, it hands over by phone or email, and refuses card numbers. Fictional data; OurAirports (public domain), NASA and NOAA open data.
