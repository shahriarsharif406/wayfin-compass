Wayfin Compass helps travellers when money problems and travel problems collide. Built for a fictional travel-card company, it guides customers through card disputes and flight disruptions instead of only answering FAQs. A pre-chat form captures the issue and a reply style, including a Simple & short mode for people who struggle with dense text. Guided workflows identify the dispute type, check the 60-day window, list the exact evidence needed, and open a real case with a case number. A flight workflow shows card travel cover plus general airline-rights information, then files a claim. When Compass lacks a reliable answer, it says so and hands over to a person by phone, email, or callback, with a summary so customers never repeat themselves. Business value: fewer long, costly dispute calls and faster resolutions.
