Wayfin Compass helps travellers when money problems and travel problems collide. Built for a fictional travel-card company, it guides customers through card disputes and flight disruptions instead of only answering FAQs. A pre-chat form captures the issue, urgency, consent, and a reply style, including a Simple & short mode for people who struggle with dense text. Guided workflows find the dispute type, list the exact evidence needed, and open a real case with a case number, priority, and status tracking. When Compass lacks a reliable answer, or a situation is urgent, it hands over to a person by phone, email, or callback, with a summary so customers never repeat themselves. Safeguards block card numbers, and a live dashboard shows self-service rate, satisfaction, and agent time saved.
