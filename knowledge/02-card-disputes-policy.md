# Wayfin Card Dispute Policy

> Fictional policy written for demonstration. Not a real bank's terms.

## When can a customer dispute a charge?
A customer can dispute a card charge within **60 days of the statement date** on which the charge first appeared. Disputes raised later are reviewed case by case but may be declined.

## Dispute types

### 1. Unauthorized transaction
The customer did not make or approve the charge and still has the card, or the card was lost, stolen, or its details were copied.
- **First step:** Freeze the card right away in the app, or ask Compass to open a case, which blocks the card.
- **Provisional credit:** For unauthorized charges up to USD 1,000, Wayfin gives a provisional credit within **5 business days** while the case is investigated.
- **Replacement card:** Sent automatically, arriving in 5 to 7 business days.
- **Evidence needed:** Date and amount of each unrecognized charge; confirmation the card was in the customer's possession (or when it went missing); a police report only if the customer reports theft and wants it on file (not required to open the case).

### 2. Duplicate charge
The same merchant charged the same amount more than once for a single purchase.
- **Evidence needed:** Dates and amounts of both charges; the merchant's name; the receipt or order confirmation showing one purchase.
- **Tip:** A pending authorization that later drops off is not a duplicate. Wait up to 7 days for pending holds to clear before disputing.

### 3. Goods or services not received
The customer paid but never received the item or service.
- **Evidence needed:** Order confirmation; the promised delivery date; proof the customer contacted the merchant (email or chat screenshot); tracking details if shipped.
- The customer should try to contact the merchant first. Wayfin can still open the case if the merchant does not respond.

### 4. Not as described or defective
The item or service was materially different from what was advertised.
- **Evidence needed:** Order confirmation; description or listing; photos of the item; proof of attempted return or merchant contact.

### 5. Wrong amount charged
The charge differs from the agreed price (for example, a tip added incorrectly, or the wrong currency applied).
- **Evidence needed:** The receipt showing the agreed amount; the amount actually charged.
- If the merchant offered to charge in the customer's home currency and the customer declined, include that detail.

### 6. Cancelled subscription still charged
The customer cancelled a recurring payment but it was charged again.
- **Evidence needed:** Date of cancellation and the merchant's cancellation confirmation.

## What is not covered
- Change of mind after a valid purchase (buyer's remorse).
- Charges the customer authorized and received as described.
- Gambling or cryptocurrency purchases.
- Charges older than the 60-day window, unless there are exceptional circumstances.

## How a case works
1. **Case opened.** The customer gets a case number in the format **WF-D-######** (for example, WF-D-104827).
2. **Review.** Wayfin contacts the merchant and reviews the evidence.
3. **Resolution timeline.** Most cases are resolved within **30 days**. Cases involving merchants outside the customer's region can take up to **90 days**.
4. **Outcome.** The customer is told by email. If the dispute succeeds, the amount is returned to the card balance. If it does not, Wayfin explains why and the customer can appeal once within 30 days.

## What if a customer is unsure of the type?
Compass should ask what happened in plain words, offer the closest type, and confirm with the customer before opening a case. If the situation does not fit any type, Compass should hand over to a person.
