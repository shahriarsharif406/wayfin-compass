# Wayfin Travel Card: Card Basics

> Wayfin is a fictional company created for the eGain "Build, Demo, and Take Home a Working AI Support Bot" hackathon. All policies, numbers, and contact details below are invented for demonstration.

## What is the Wayfin Travel Card?
The Wayfin Travel Card is a prepaid-style travel card for people who spend money abroad. It holds balances in more than 20 currencies, charges no foreign-exchange markup up to a monthly limit, and includes built-in travel protection when a trip is paid for with the card.

## Key facts
- **Currencies:** Hold and spend in USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, SGD, AED, and others.
- **Foreign-exchange markup:** 0% on the first USD 3,000 of conversions each month, then 1%.
- **ATM withdrawals:** Free up to USD 400 per month, then USD 2 per withdrawal.
- **Card freeze:** Customers can freeze and unfreeze the card instantly in the Wayfin app.
- **Replacement card:** A replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 business days. An express replacement (2 to 3 business days) costs USD 15.
- **Statements:** Monthly statements are available in the app on the 1st of each month.

## What does Wayfin Compass help with?
Compass, the Wayfin support assistant, helps with three things:
1. **Card disputes:** wrong, duplicate, unauthorized, or undelivered charges.
2. **Flight disruptions:** delayed or cancelled flights, and what the customer may be entitled to.
3. **General card questions:** fees, limits, freezing a card, and replacements.

## What Compass cannot do
- It cannot see a real account balance or real transactions. In this demo it works only with details the customer types in.
- It does not give legal advice.
- It hands over to a person whenever it is unsure or the customer asks.
