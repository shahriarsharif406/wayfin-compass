# Flight Disruptions and Wayfin Travel Cover

> Fictional Wayfin policy plus plain-language summaries of public passenger-rights rules. The summaries are simplified and for general information only, not legal advice. Rules change, so customers should check the official sources named below.

## Part A: Wayfin Travel Cover (fictional product benefits)
These benefits apply only when the **flight or trip was paid for with the Wayfin Travel Card**.

### Flight delay cover
- If a flight is delayed by **4 hours or more**, Wayfin reimburses meals and refreshments up to **USD 75 per day**, with receipts.

### Flight cancellation cover
- If the flight is cancelled and the airline does not refund or rebook, Wayfin reimburses prepaid, non-refundable trip costs (such as hotel nights) up to **USD 1,500 per trip**.

### Delayed baggage cover
- If checked baggage is delayed **6 hours or more**, Wayfin reimburses essential items (toiletries, basic clothing) up to **USD 200**, with receipts.

### Claim rules
- Claims must be filed within **30 days** of the disruption.
- Required documents: booking confirmation or ticket showing payment with the Wayfin card; proof of the delay or cancellation (airline notice or email); receipts for expenses.
- Case numbers follow the format **WF-F-######** (for example, WF-F-208315).
- Wayfin cover is separate from and additional to what the airline owes. Customers should claim from the airline first where the airline is responsible.
- Wayfin does not cover disruptions the customer caused (such as missing a flight) or disruptions known before the card purchase.

## Part B: Airline obligations, plain-language summaries

### European Union and UK rules (EU261 and the UK equivalent)
These can apply when:
- the flight departed from an EU or UK airport (any airline), **or**
- the flight arrived in the EU (or UK, under UK rules) on an airline based in the EU (or UK).

**Compensation** may be owed when the flight arrives **3 hours or more late**, or is cancelled with less than 14 days' notice, **unless** the airline shows "extraordinary circumstances" (for example, severe weather, air-traffic control strikes, or security risks). Typical fixed amounts depend on distance:
- up to 1,500 km: EUR 250
- 1,500 km to 3,500 km (or longer flights within the EU): EUR 400
- over 3,500 km: EUR 600

The airline must also provide **care** during long delays (meals, refreshments, communication, and hotel when needed) and offer a **refund or re-routing** when a flight is cancelled.

Official sources: the European Commission air passenger rights page and the UK Civil Aviation Authority.

### United States rules (Department of Transportation)
- US law does not require airlines to pay cash compensation for delays.
- Passengers are entitled to an **automatic refund** to the original form of payment when the airline cancels a flight, or makes a significant change, and the passenger chooses not to travel. Significant delay thresholds are set by the Department of Transportation.
- Airlines must publish their own customer service commitments for meals and hotels.

Official source: the US Department of Transportation "Aviation Consumer Protection" pages.

### Other regions
Many countries have their own rules. If the flight is outside the EU, UK, and US, Compass should explain only Wayfin Travel Cover and suggest the customer check their national aviation authority.

## Part C: What Compass should and should not say
- Compass **may** explain which rules could apply and what documents to collect.
- Compass **must not** promise that a specific airline will pay.
- Compass should say clearly when a case is uncertain and offer a human agent.
