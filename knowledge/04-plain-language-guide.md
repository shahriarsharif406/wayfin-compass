# Plain-Language Guide (Simple and Short Mode)

> This file is the "simple mode" version of the key policies, written in short sentences for customers who choose Simple & short replies. It is meant for people who find long or technical text hard to read, including people with cognitive or learning disabilities, people reading in a second language, and older adults.

## Rules for Simple & short mode
- One idea per sentence.
- Maximum 15 words per sentence where possible.
- Maximum 4 steps at a time. Then ask "Ready for the next step?"
- No jargon. Say "pending charge" as "a charge that is not final yet."
- Always end with one clear question or one clear next step.

## Disputes in simple words

**Can I dispute a charge?**
Yes. You have 60 days. The time starts from the statement date.

**What if I did not make the charge?**
First, freeze your card in the app. Then I can open a case for you. We will send a new card. It takes 5 to 7 working days. We may give your money back for now within 5 working days.

**What if I was charged twice?**
Wait up to 7 days. Some charges go away on their own. If both charges stay, tell me. I will open a case.

**What if I never got my item?**
Please try the shop first. Keep your order email. If the shop does not answer, I can open a case.

**How long does a case take?**
Most cases take up to 30 days. Some take up to 90 days.

## Flight problems in simple words

**My flight was late. What can I get?**
It depends on your trip. If you paid with a Wayfin card, you may get money for meals. The flight must be 4 hours late or more. You need receipts.

**My flight was cancelled. What can I get?**
First, ask the airline for a refund or a new flight. If you paid with Wayfin, we may also pay some trip costs. We pay up to USD 1,500.

**What papers do I need?**
- Your booking or ticket.
- A message from the airline about the delay or cancellation.
- Receipts for what you paid.

**How long do I have?**
You have 30 days to tell us.

## Getting a person
If you want to talk to a person, say "person". I will give you phone and email choices. I will also pass on a short note so you do not repeat your story.
