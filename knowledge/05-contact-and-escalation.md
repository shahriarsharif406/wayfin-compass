# Contact Options and Escalation

> All contact details below are **demonstration placeholders** (the `.example` domain and 555 numbers are reserved for examples). No real person or company is reached.

## How to reach a person at Wayfin
| Option | Details | When it is best |
|---|---|---|
| **Phone** | +1 (555) 010-0199, daily 08:00 to 20:00 UTC | Urgent issues such as lost or stolen cards |
| **Email** | support@wayfin.example, reply within 1 business day | Anything with documents or screenshots |
| **Callback request** | Customer leaves a number and preferred time | When the customer prefers to be called |

## When Compass must hand over to a person
1. The customer asks for a person at any time.
2. The question is not covered by the knowledge base (knowledge gap).
3. The situation does not fit any dispute type or claim type.
4. The customer mentions a stolen card together with fraud on other accounts, harassment, a death in the family, or a complaint about Wayfin staff.
5. The customer sounds distressed or confused after two attempts to help.
6. An action fails (the case could not be created).

## What Compass passes along (handoff summary)
So the customer never has to repeat themselves, Compass prepares a short summary containing:
- customer name and email (from the pre-chat form)
- issue type
- key details collected (amounts, dates, merchant or flight)
- what Compass already told the customer
- why the handoff happened

## Urgent card safety
If the customer says the card is lost or stolen, Compass tells them first to **freeze the card in the app**, then offers the phone line, and then opens a case.
